Farming Guidance

Useful tips and practices for sustainable and profitable farming.

🌱 General Farming Tips

  • Always check soil quality before planting crops.
  • Rotate crops to maintain soil fertility.
  • Use organic manure to reduce chemical dependency.
  • Adopt modern techniques like drip irrigation and greenhouse farming.

🌾 Crop Selection

Choose crops according to soil type, climate, and local demand:

  • Wheat, Barley → Suitable for loamy soil, cold climate.
  • Paddy (Rice) → Requires clayey soil and abundant water.
  • Pulses → Grow well in sandy-loam soil.
  • Cash Crops (Sugarcane, Cotton) → High water and nutrient demand.

💧 Fertilizer Guidance

Balanced use of fertilizers ensures better yield and soil health:

  • Conduct Soil Testing before applying fertilizers.
  • Use Organic Manure & Compost for eco-friendly farming.
  • Avoid excessive use of Urea, balance it with Potash & Phosphates.
  • Neem-coated urea prevents nitrogen loss.

🚰 Irrigation Methods

Adopt efficient irrigation methods to save water:

  • Drip Irrigation → Saves up to 70% water, ideal for fruit & vegetable crops.
  • Sprinkler Irrigation → Suitable for sandy soils.
  • Rainwater Harvesting → Store rainwater for irrigation during dry seasons.

🐛 Pest & Disease Control

Minimize crop losses by controlling pests in eco-friendly ways:

  • Use Neem oil spray as a natural pesticide.
  • Adopt Integrated Pest Management (IPM) practices.
  • Introduce friendly insects like ladybugs to control aphids.
  • Avoid excessive chemical sprays to protect soil and human health.
